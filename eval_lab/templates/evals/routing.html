How the router picks a model

  1. Count the tokens (Claude's and Gemini's free counters, else an estimate). Haiku never gets a prompt over 100,000 tokens.
  2. Rules decide the complexity of most jobs; a cheap tagger model decides only the unclear ones.
  3. The routing table below gives the model for that job and complexity; without a row, the Settings choice; without that, the step's default.
  4. If the answer fails the quick check, the next model up the ladder tries again. Busy free models fall back to their listed alternatives.
  5. Real customer text never goes to a free model or to Gemini unless the business allows it; local-only mode uses Ollama models only.

Edit the table in Settings, Routing.
