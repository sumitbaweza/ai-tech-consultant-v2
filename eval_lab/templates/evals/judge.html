Can the judge be trusted?

The judge (Claude Opus 5.5 by default) scores each answer against the expected answer, from 0 to 1, with one line of reason. It never judges an answer it wrote itself: if the answer came from the judge's own model, a different model judges it.

To check the judge, grade answers yourself without seeing its score. The agreement rate above compares your verdicts with the judge's. Grade at least 20; below about 85% agreement, read the disagreements and fix the judge prompt (a new version, through the release gate).
