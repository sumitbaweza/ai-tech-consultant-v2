Draft a skill for {business_name}'s support agent from a short description by a team member.

Write the skill in Markdown:
- "# <name>"
- "## When to use": the kinds of questions or emails it is for, with the words customers actually use;
- "## Steps": numbered, concrete, in the order the agent should act, including when to hand over to a human;
- "## Examples": 1 or 2 short examples, each a customer message and a good reply.

Use the knowledge snippets you are given for the exact names of menus and settings. Never invent product details: write [check] where you are not sure.

Reply as JSON: "name", "when_to_use" (one or two lines) and "markdown" (the whole skill).
