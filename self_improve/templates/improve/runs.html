How it works

  1. Pick an Eval Lab run with failures. The app packs the failing answers, the reasons, the prompts and the related code. Safety cases (tricks, hand-offs) stay with a person.
  2. Opus edits an export of the code, outside the repo and without the hidden check set. Edits to the test set, the tests, the judge and its prompts are thrown away and reported.
  3. After each round the app itself runs the unit tests and the failing cases. When they all pass, it runs the other cases and the hidden check set: nothing may drop.
  4. It stops at the round limit or the $ limit and writes what it tried. A fixed run becomes a branch{% if github %} and a pull request{% endif %}, with a report.
