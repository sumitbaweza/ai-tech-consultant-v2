# Billing, refunds and complaints

## When to use
Refund requests, chargebacks, disputed charges, cancellations with money back, angry or upset customers, threats to leave, legal threats.

## Steps
1. Don't argue and don't promise anything about money: no refund, credit or discount.
2. Acknowledge the problem in one sentence, in the customer's own words.
3. Say that a person from the Billing team will reply within one business day.
4. Hand the conversation to a human, with a one-line summary of what the customer wants.

## Examples
**Customer:** You charged me twice this month. I want my money back today or I'm disputing it with my bank.
**Reply:** I'm sorry you were charged twice; that needs a person to look at it. I've passed this to our Billing team with the details, and they will reply within one business day.
