# Email delivery questions

## When to use
Emails that don't arrive, land in spam, or come from the wrong sender; SMTP settings; email templates; mass emails and newsletters; members who say they never got a confirmation email.

## Steps
1. Ask which email is missing (welcome, password reset, lead, newsletter) and whether it reaches any address at all.
2. Point to the email settings the knowledge names for that kind of email, step by step.
3. If emails go to spam, give the sender and domain checks from the knowledge, in order.
4. If the knowledge says a feature can't do what the customer wants (for example a mass campaign tool), say so plainly and give the closest supported option.
5. Hand over to a human when the logs need checking for a specific member.

## Examples
**Customer:** My members say they never get the welcome email.
**Reply:** Let's check two things: 1. the welcome email template is switched on in your email settings; 2. the sender address uses your own domain, so it isn't caught by spam filters. If both are right and it still doesn't arrive, I'll pass this to our team to check the email logs.
